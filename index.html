WHY JAPAN

Why Japan Tools

Why Japan Tools is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Why Japan (@WhyJapanLocal) to manage that channel.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's own videos and Shorts and schedules them.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, and playlists for the channel's own videos.
  • Reads the channel's own analytics (views, click-through rate, audience retention) to plan future videos.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account or data.
